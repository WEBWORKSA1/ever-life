    What is biological age?

    Biological age is an estimate of how old your body seems compared with the average person of your calendar age. Researchers measure it with blood markers, fitness tests and DNA methylation "clocks". This quiz uses lifestyle factors that are strongly associated with healthy ageing, so it is a fast, free way to see where you stand and what to work on.

    Lab-based biological age tests vary widely between companies and are still being validated, so treat any single number with caution. Our guide to longevity biomarkers explains which numbers have the strongest evidence.

    Note: This quiz is educational and not a diagnosis. Speak with a health professional before making major changes.
