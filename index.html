Questions, answered

Is Ever.Life free?

Yes. Every tool and guide is free and needs no account. We are supported by advertising, reader contributions and partner professionals who pay to be introduced to people who request help.

Are the calculators accurate?

They use published population averages and well-studied risk factors, so they are useful for understanding direction and scale. They are not personal medical or financial advice.

What happens when I request a match?

Your request goes to our team, who connect you with a licensed professional in your region for the service you chose. There is no cost and no obligation.

Do you sell insurance or legal services?

No. Ever.Life is an independent publisher. We are not an insurer, agent, broker, law firm or medical provider.

Is Ever.Life connected to any company called EverLife?

No. Ever.Life is an independent publication and is not affiliated with any company, product or trademark using a similar name. See our trademark notice.
